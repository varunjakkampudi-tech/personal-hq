Health basics

Safe starting points for your plan

Your workout and diet should fit your health, goals, food preferences and schedule. Until you share those, this hub keeps it general: regular meals, protein-rich foods, vegetables and fruit, enough water, gradual training, rest days, and sleep. For medical conditions, injuries, or major diet changes, get guidance from a qualified clinician or dietitian.
