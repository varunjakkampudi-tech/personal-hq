# Career transition workbook · February 2027 target

**Purpose:** Prepare accurate resume and profile updates for a February 2027 company move. This is a blank planning checklist, not a resume, profile submission, or job application.

**Privacy:** Keep completed personal values, the actual resume, contact details, compensation, work authorization, references, and private company/client information in a secure personal location. Do not commit filled answers, passwords, OTPs, government IDs, or account recovery details to this repo. Share only the fields needed for a specific review. Redact confidential client, product, customer, and employer data before using examples.

**Foundit privacy gate:** Foundit's current privacy notice says it may collect publicly available personal information from unregistered users and third-party sources, including LinkedIn and Facebook, to make profiles accessible to potential employers. Registration may not be the first point at which public profile data is processed. Before using Foundit, check whether a profile already exists, clarify current opt-out, correction and deletion routes with Foundit, and proceed only if this exposure is acceptable. Recheck the live notice and controls at setup.

**Wellfound privacy gate:** Wellfound says its Public Profile remains viewable even when the Jobs Profile is closed. Recruiter visibility for the Jobs Profile depends on job-search settings or applications, and current-employer exclusion depends on the current role being marked accurately. The official help page was last updated 23 February 2023, so recheck live settings before setup.
