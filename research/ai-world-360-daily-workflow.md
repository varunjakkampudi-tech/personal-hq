# AI World 360 · daily publishing workflow and log

**Goal:** Prepare one original video per day for YouTube and Instagram, with platform-specific exports from a shared source project where practical. A video is complete only after publication is confirmed on both intended channels and its URLs are recorded. The content format, niche, daily publishing time, editing setup and available production hours are not yet documented; do not assume them.

**Tool order:** Use vidIQ and ChatGPT for title, description, tags and publishing metadata; use ChatGPT for the thumbnail/cover concept; use Google Flow for video generation. Keep the edit, captions and platform-specific exports in the selected editing tool. Preserve the user's review before publishing. A saved draft alone does not approve schedule activation; get any required final confirmation for the specific draft and schedule.

## Daily production checklist

1. **Select the topic** — Check vidIQ for search interest and competing coverage. Record the chosen angle and why it fits the channel. Use current primary/credible sources for factual or news-driven claims; record source links and fact-check dates.
2. **Write the concept and script** — Draft a clear promise, opening hook, sequence of points, visual plan and call to action. Keep claims within the source evidence. Mark opinions, estimates and sponsored/affiliate relationships accurately.
3. **Review the script** — Check factual claims, names, numbers, pronunciation, originality, channel voice, audience fit and any sensitive or potentially harmful content. Do not use confidential or personal material without approval.
4. **Generate visual assets** — Create the approved video/scene assets in Google Flow from the reviewed brief. Record the model/version if available and preserve the project/source assets privately. Verify rights or permitted usage for all third-party footage, music, images, voices and logos.
5. **Edit and make accessible** — Produce captions/subtitles, check spelling and timing, balance audio, confirm readability and safe areas, and review the complete exported video with sound. Do not assume the same export is compliant for both platforms.
6. **Package each platform** — Use vidIQ and ChatGPT to draft and compare the YouTube title, description, tags and metadata; use ChatGPT to prepare the thumbnail. Prepare an Instagram caption and cover. Confirm each platform's current format, length, file and publishing requirements at export time rather than hard-coding limits here.
7. **Review final assets** — Watch/listen to each platform export from start to finish. Compare title, thumbnail/cover, captions, disclosure, links and CTA with the video. Record reviewer and review time. Fix defects before upload.
8. **Publish or schedule** — Upload the approved export to each intended channel. Before activating a scheduled post, record the specific approved asset, channel, date/time and approval. If the user has not approved that exact schedule, leave it as a draft and carry the item forward.
9. **Verify and learn** — Confirm the post appears on each channel and save both public URLs and actual publish times. Record available 24-hour and 7-day views, watch/retention signal, click-through signal where supplied, engagement and one learning. Do not invent analytics or treat a draft as published.

## Carry-forward rules

- Keep every unfinished item in the queue until its blocker is resolved or the user explicitly cancels it. Each daily review should show today's next action and any overdue item, while preserving the dated production history.
- Do not count an idea, generated clip, edited draft or scheduled post as published. A daily item is complete only when both intended channels have verified live URLs, unless the user changes the channel plan for that item.
- Use **Blocked** only with a specific blocker and next action; do not create a duplicate item for the same video.
- Do not delete yesterday's record to make a new daily summary. Refresh the summary and append the next day's item.

## Set up before the first production day

- [ ] Confirm niche, audience, language(s), recurring series, voice and visual identity.
- [ ] Confirm whether the daily target is one short/reel cross-posted to both channels or distinct YouTube and Instagram cuts.
- [ ] Confirm the usual publish time and time zone, working hours and batch-production preference.
- [ ] Capture the YouTube and Instagram channel URLs and check access using the approved sign-in method; never add passwords or OTPs to the HQ.
- [ ] Confirm the final-review and schedule-approval handoff for each video.
- [ ] Set a consistent private location for scripts, Flow projects, edits, exports and thumbnails; store only references in Personal HQ.
