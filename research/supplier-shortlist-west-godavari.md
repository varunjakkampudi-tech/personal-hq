## Solar power and solar-enabled CCTV

First decide whether “solar CCTV” means a small independent solar supply for cameras, a farm-wide PV system that also powers cameras, or both. System capacity and batteries cannot be selected responsibly until the site, electrical loads, grid reliability, outage duration, water-pump duty, milking equipment and camera/network plan are known.

Use the vendor shortlist as a request-for-quote queue, not as a recommendation to purchase. Ask each solar bidder for a site-specific single-line diagram, itemized equipment schedule, generation assumptions, battery autonomy assumptions, earthing and surge protection, mounting/wind design, commissioning records, warranties, maintenance scope, GST details, and references that can be called. Do not assume a rooftop subsidy applies to a farm/commercial system.

Prefer a dairy planner/structural engineer-led layout over a generic shed quote. Request two drawings: a phase-one 20-animal plan and an expansion-ready 40-animal site plan. Have a qualified local professional verify the structural design, wind actions, foundations, permissions and estimate before signing.

## Evidence-based design checks

- Tamil Nadu Agricultural University’s cattle and buffalo housing guidance says to account for water, electricity, drainage, local climate, a flexible layout, animal class, roof ventilation and herd-size changes. It describes 20–50-animal double-row arrangements as an example; it is planning guidance, not a site-specific design or a substitute for Andhra Pradesh approvals.
- The West Godavari dairy guide says no plot survey, local supplier quote or site visit has been done. This shortlist does not change that status.
- Do not select a system from advertised price per square foot or nameplate solar kW alone. Compare like-for-like scope, equipment, warranties, exclusions, taxes, service and references.

## Quote comparison template

For every bidder, capture: legal business name and GSTIN; named technical lead; visit date; project references with customer permission to contact; scope and exclusions; drawings; material/brand/model/quantity; structural or electrical calculations; price with tax and payment milestones; delivery schedule; warranty and service SLAs; insurance; permits; and defects/retention terms. Score technical compliance and references before price. No purchase or contract decision is recorded here.
