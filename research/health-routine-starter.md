# Personal health and routine starter

Status: starter guidance only; not a personalized medical or nutrition plan.

## What is known and unknown

This workspace does not yet have the user's age, medical history, injuries, current activity level, training experience, sleep schedule, dietary preferences, allergies, or specific fitness goal. Do not set a calorie target, weight target, supplement plan, or intense training prescription until those details are supplied. If there is a chronic condition, significant pain, recent surgery, pregnancy, or a long period of inactivity, seek clinician guidance before increasing exercise.

## Safe starting structure

- Start with comfortable walking or other moderate movement and build gradually toward the WHO adult guideline of 150–300 minutes per week; some activity is better than none.
- Include muscle-strengthening activity on at least two days each week, adjusted to ability and recovery. Stop for chest pain, faintness, unusual shortness of breath, or sharp pain and seek appropriate care.
- Use regular meals built around varied vegetables and fruit, pulses/beans, whole grains or millets, and suitable protein foods. Prefer minimally processed foods, drink to thirst and conditions, and avoid turning this into a restrictive diet.
- Track only a few useful signals initially: movement minutes, strength sessions, sleep window, energy, and any symptoms. Review weekly; adjust one thing at a time.

## Details needed to personalize

1. Main goal: strength, general fitness, fat loss, mobility, stamina, or another goal.
2. Age range and current weekly activity; gym/home equipment and available days/minutes.
3. Any health conditions, medications, injuries, movement restrictions, or clinician advice.
4. Typical sleep/work schedule and stress/recovery constraints.
5. Food pattern (vegetarian/eggetarian/non-vegetarian), allergies, foods avoided, meal timing, and cooking constraints.
6. What should be tracked, and what should remain private.

## Sources

- WHO, [Physical activity](https://www.who.int/initiatives/behealthy/physical-activity): adult activity recommendations.
- ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition, [Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024](https://nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjs/locale/DGI_2024.pdf): India-specific general dietary guidance.

These sources inform broad starter guidance only; they do not determine an individualized plan.
