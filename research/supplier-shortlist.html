Before comparing equipment or construction prices

Solar sizing: first record pump, milking, cooling, lighting, fans, CCTV/network loads and outage duration. Compare grid-tied, hybrid backup and dedicated camera supply only where appropriate. Request drawings, generation/battery assumptions, equipment schedule, earthing/surge protection, mounting/wind design, commissioning, warranties, maintenance, exclusions and tax.

CCTV scope: draw coverage for gates, animal areas, stores, milking/utility zones and approach points; mark private living areas off-limits. Specify night image quality, recording days, UPS/solar runtime, network path, secure remote access, warranty and service response. Test after dark before acceptance.

Shed scope: request a phase-one 20-animal plan and an expansion-ready 40-animal site plan. Price site preparation, floors, drainage, feed/water, animal grouping, calving/calf space, sick/quarantine area, manure handling, storage, milking/utility rooms and staff movement separately.

Confirm animal species/mix, village, site survey, soil, flood/drainage conditions, water and power before fixing dimensions. Have a qualified local professional verify design, wind actions, foundations, permissions and estimate.

Quote comparison checklist

  • Legal business name, GSTIN, named technical lead and local service contact
  • Two comparable completed farm references; ask permission to call the owners
  • Site-visit date, drawings, design calculations and itemized bill of materials
  • Brands, models, quantities, exclusions, taxes, payment stages and delivery schedule
  • Warranty, maintenance, support response, insurance and defects/retention terms
  • Score technical fit and reference quality before comparing prices
